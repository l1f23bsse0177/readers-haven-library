Registration Process

How to Apply for Membership

Registering for your Readers' Haven Library Card is straightforward and takes less than fifteen minutes at our front reception.

01

Complete Form

Fill out the basic registration form at our reception desk or download and print our standard application form.

02

Present Documents

Provide your valid CNIC, Passport, or Student ID card alongside a recent residential utility bill for address verification.

03

Pay Annual Fee

Settle the annual subsidized civic membership fee via cash, debit card, or online bank transfer at our service desk.

04

Receive Your Card

Collect your laminated physical smart library card and activate your online catalogue PIN immediately.

Library Governance

Borrowing Guidelines & Return Policies

To ensure all community members have fair and equitable access to our shared collection, we kindly ask patrons to observe these stewardship rules.

Borrowing Guidelines

Card Presentation: Members must present their physical or digital library barcode card upon checking out materials at self-service kiosks or circulation desks.

Renewals: Items may be renewed up to twice online or over the telephone, provided no other member has placed an active hold on the title.

Reference Stacks: Rare manuscripts, dictionaries, current print periodicals, and archival reference works are restricted to in-library use only.

Inter-Library Requests: Members in good standing can request up to 3 external volumes per month through our reciprocal lending consortium.

Late Returns & Item Care

Grace Period: We offer a 3-day courteous grace period following the official due date before any overdue administrative charges accrue.

Overdue Fee: A nominal community fee of PKR 20 per day per overdue volume applies thereafter, capped at PKR 500 per item to encourage return.

Automated Reminders: Courteous reminder emails and SMS notifications are dispatched 3 days prior to the due date and on the due date.

Lost or Damaged Items: Lost volumes require reimbursement of the publisher's replacement price plus a PKR 300 processing and cataloguing fee.
