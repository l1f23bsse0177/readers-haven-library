How to Access Our Services

Whether visiting in person or connecting remotely, accessing our resources is streamlined for all community members.

1. Check In at Front Desk

First-time visitors can obtain a daily guest pass or register for full membership by presenting national ID or student identification at our welcome hall.

2. Use Self-Service Kiosks

Interactive touchscreen stations located at the entrance of each floor allow you to locate books, check queue reservations, and book study rooms instantly.

3. Ask a Reference Librarian

Our professional research staff are stationed on each floor to provide research citations, manuscript handling assistance, and technical help.
